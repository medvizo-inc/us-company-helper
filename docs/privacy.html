Privacy

Updated 3 November 2026

What it stores

What you type into the extension: your company name, EIN, corporate address, US phone number, and the name and value of any field you add.

Where it is stored

In your browser's local storage for the extension (chrome.storage.local), on the computer you typed it on. It is not synced to your Google account or to other computers.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. Nobody, BaseAcross included, receives what you store. The extension asks for one permission, storage, and cannot read or change the websites you visit.

Copying

When you press Copy, the value is put on your computer's clipboard, where any app you paste into can read it, as with anything you copy.

Deleting

Clear a field, remove a field you added, or remove the extension from Chrome to delete everything it stored.

Questions

Ask through baseacross.com/contact. BaseAcross's privacy notice for its own website and service is at baseacross.com/privacy.
